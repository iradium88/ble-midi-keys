Help & credits

Bluetooth: turn the microKEY Air on in Bluetooth mode, tap Bluetooth, and pick it from the list. Don't pair it in Android's Bluetooth settings first; Chrome connects to it directly.

USB: plug the keyboard in (on a phone, with a USB OTG adapter), tap USB and allow MIDI access. After that the page picks up the keyboard by itself whenever it's plugged in.
